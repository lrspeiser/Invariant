# 02. A correlation whose sign belonged to the estimator

**Where:** Run K.2 (retraction)

## What was claimed

An environmental correlation of rho_p = -0.304.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| the number that looked real | **-0.304** |
| the same number under control | **the sign did not survive re-derivation** |

## Why it happened

The estimator itself carried a sign preference that was mistaken for a property of the sky.

## What caught it

Re-derive the estimator independently before quoting its sign.

## The lesson

Recorded because retraction is the rarest outcome in this catalogue. The programme record carries the retraction next to the original claim rather than replacing it.

**Cost of the control:** One re-derivation.
