# 07. A cosmic axis made entirely of shot noise

**Where:** Run AP

## What was claimed

A preferred global axis in the well network.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| the number that looked real | **growth anisotropy 12.4 at 100 wells** |
| the same number under control | **1.10 at 100,000 wells -- the amplitude is set by particle count** |

## Why it happened

The tensor estimators manufactured a global axis out of catalogue shot noise. In the continuum limit the effect is exactly zero; the ensemble quadrupole falls as roughly N^-0.557.

## What caught it

Vary the sample size and watch the amplitude.

## The lesson

A real anisotropy is indifferent to how many objects you used. A shot-noise anisotropy is a function of it.

**Cost of the control:** Re-running the same estimator at three sample sizes.
