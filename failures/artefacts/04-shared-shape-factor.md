# 04. Both axes contained the same term

**Where:** Run Z.4

## What was claimed

A correlation between a shape factor and a gravitational quantity.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| the number that looked real | **a confident correlation of plausible size** |
| the same number under control | **the correlation is a restatement of the shared term** |

## Why it happened

The quantity being correlated appeared inside the construction expression of BOTH axes. The correlation measured the sharing, not the physics.

## What caught it

Write down the construction expression for each axis and look for common factors, before quoting any correlation.

## The lesson

This check needs no compute and no data. It takes minutes and it has now caught two of the nine.

**Cost of the control:** Minutes, with a pen.
