The problem ledger

Every target this machine works on is declared here first, in a sealed queue — a hash-bound registry that cannot be edited without changing its fingerprint. Each entry cites a source, says in plain prose why the question is believed open, and defines in advance what would count as progress, so no result can quietly move its own goalposts. Calibration entries — questions with known answers, or manufactured worlds — carry their labels as validated schema fields, which means a rediscovery can never be dressed up as a discovery by dropping a sentence. Queue membership asserts provenance, nothing more: not importance, not tractability, not solvability.

Calibration of exactly this character has been run at scale once already: a sealed campaign of twelve blinded rediscoveries, written up paper by paper — each with its receipt-derived elimination funnel — under Papers.
