The question

MOND-like acceleration laws that fit galaxy rotation curves underpredict the dynamical mass of galaxy clusters by roughly a factor of two; no baryons-only law is known to close the gap.

Where it comes from

R. H. Sanders, The Virial Discrepancy in Clusters of Galaxies in the Context of Modified Newtonian Dynamics, Astrophys. J. 512, L23 (1999).

Why it sits in the queue

Sanders (1999) documents the residual factor ~2 mass discrepancy in clusters under MOND, and the subsequent literature has not produced a baryons-only law that fits both galaxies and clusters.

What counts as progress

Expected outcome is a sealed negative: candidate laws that pass the galaxy gates are evaluated on cluster mass data and the underprediction is receipted exactly. Progress = that sealed verdict, or a law family that measurably narrows the factor-2 gap without per-cluster freedom.
