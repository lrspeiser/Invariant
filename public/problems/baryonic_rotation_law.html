Statement

Find a universal law nu(y) mapping the baryonic gravitational acceleration g_bar to the observed acceleration g_obs = nu(g_bar/a0) * g_bar that reproduces flat outer rotation curves and the baryonic Tully-Fisher relation from baryons alone.

Source citation

S. S. McGaugh, F. Lelli, J. M. Schombert, Radial Acceleration Relation in Rotationally Supported Galaxies, Phys. Rev. Lett. 117, 201101 (2016).

Why it is believed open

McGaugh-Lelli-Schombert (2016) establish the radial acceleration relation empirically across 153 galaxies, but no derivation of a unique nu(y) from baryons alone is accepted; whether the relation reflects modified dynamics or dark-matter physics is unresolved.

What counts as progress

Progress = a nu(y) candidate that passes the full gate ladder (Newtonian limit, flatness, BTFR slope, positivity) beyond the 1,129,900,996-candidate GPU screen receipted at runs/gpu-baryonic-screen/billion-v1.json, and then survives the sealed validation ladder; per-galaxy free parameters are inadmissible.
