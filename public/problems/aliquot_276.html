The question

The aliquot sequence starting at 276, iterating s(n) = (sum of divisors of n) - n, is not known to terminate, enter a cycle, or diverge; its boundedness is unknown.

Where it comes from

R. K. Guy, Unsolved Problems in Number Theory, 3rd ed., Springer, 2004, problem B6 (aliquot sequences).

Why it sits in the queue

Guy B6 records the Catalan-Dickson question as open and 276 as the smallest starting value whose aliquot sequence has never been shown bounded or unbounded despite thousands of computed terms.

What counts as progress

Progress = an exact invariant, growth law, or congruence structure of the computed trajectory that survives holdout, or a proved boundedness or unboundedness criterion applicable to 276; extending the raw trajectory is not progress.
