# Gravity roadmap Item 15 — timescale-ratio synthesis

## Decision

**`REJECT_ITEM15_PROMOTION_RETAIN_TIMESCALE_HINT_ADVANCE_ITEM16`**

Item 15 is complete in its frozen scope. Two materially different real-data searches point in a
favorable direction, but neither survives its search-aware significance gate and they do not
produce one stable cross-scale formula. The result is a bounded positive hint, not a promoted
law. All 85 confirmation objects remain sealed.

The shared observation is only directional: nonlinear clock organization can modestly improve
held-out prediction in both lanes. It is not the same response, the same objects, or the same
exact equation. Similar `p` values do not combine into a discovery because the formulas and
tests were not a preregistered common-law meta-analysis.

## Material improvement over where Item 15 started

Before attempt 2, Item 15 could not answer whether a real cooling time helped; the galaxy source
contained only dynamical, mass-growth, relaxation-null, and cosmic proxies and failed its quality
floor. The second lane adds:

- published direct cooling times derived from measured density, temperature, and cooling
  functions;
- baryon-only free-fall, sound-crossing, and cosmic clocks at three fixed physical radii;
- a genuinely independent cluster population and lensing-related response;
- an explicit contamination remedy excluding every system in a prematurely accessed 100-cluster
  response source;
- an exact 18/5 exploration/confirmation boundary frozen before any LC2 mass access;
- 262,144 provenance-labeled program cells and 99 full nested-selection null reruns on the RTX
  5090;
- subgroup, source-family, and measurement-error robustness checks.

That is a material improvement in testing capability and evidence discipline. It is not a
material improvement in our ability to claim a new gravity law: the decisive significance gate
still fails.

## Why the hint is retained

The galaxy lane improves the primary and fixed secondary tracer, although it regresses in slow
growth and higher-mass subsets. The cleaner cluster lane improves all frozen cooling,
temperature, redshift, and lensing-source slices. Four of five cluster folds independently favor
a localized cooling-threshold-shell family.

This makes the direction worth preserving in the failure/lead archive. It should not be pruned
merely because the exact first test is imperfect.

## Why it is not promoted or confirmed

- Galaxy quality retains only 123 objects versus the frozen 150 and 62.5% floors.
- Galaxy `p=0.225`; cluster `p=0.22` after repeating the entire selection inside the null.
- The cluster sample contains only 18 exploration systems.
- The cluster error-weighted gain is only 1.37%.
- One cluster fold selects a known direct ratio rewrite rather than a qualifying synthesis.
- The other folds disagree on radius, threshold, modulation, and one coefficient sign.
- LC2 `M500` is heterogeneous and model-dependent, not direct shear or strong-lensing images.
- Gas inside 100 kpc omits stars and most outer gas, so the target is not total gravity divided by
  total baryons.

Opening the confirmation sets now would spend independent evidence on a formula that is not yet
fixed. Retuning the opened responses would turn a useful hint into overfit evidence.

## What this means for the age/baryonic-mass idea

These results do not show that age changes how many baryons exist. They are consistent with
several possibilities that remain unresolved:

1. cooling or population state changes how well light and X-rays trace baryonic mass;
2. dynamical state changes geometry or equilibrium assumptions;
3. cooling ratios correlate with a separate cluster property such as mass, concentration,
   selection, or lensing calibration;
4. a real history- or state-dependent field effect exists.

The strong baseline controls the measured raw ingredients, which makes option 3 less trivial,
but an 18-object, `p=0.22` result cannot distinguish those explanations. Direct shear/images and
a much more complete baryonic inventory are required later.

## Scope of the rejection

Rejected for promotion are the exact two frozen scalar-timescale grammars and their selected
cells. Not rejected are action-derived temporal fields, causal baryonic-history kernels,
nonlocal clocks, complete baryon models, direct-lensing predictions, or a later theory that
independently derives a fixed ratio without looking at these responses.

Across both attempts the system froze 524,288 formula cells, performed 10,082,058,240
candidate-observation score evaluations including the cluster null, used zero paid model calls,
generated zero post-response formulas, and opened zero confirmation responses.
