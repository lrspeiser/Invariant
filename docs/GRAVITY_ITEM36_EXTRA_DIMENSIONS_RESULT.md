## Claim boundary and next action

This experiment does not rule out extra dimensions. It tests four screened static radial
Green-function projections over four MaNGA annuli. It does not solve the bulk metric, derive
cosmology, prove the full action or stability, predict gravitational slip or null geodesics, or
test cluster lensing. The narrower result is that the search machinery is identifiable and
computationally sound, but the selected DGP-like projection is much less predictive than ordinary
radial source controls on the usable galaxies, and the intended H-alpha sample is too incomplete
for a formal family verdict.

Keep the 40 confirmations sealed. Preserve all four Item 36 regions, 58,774 behavioral classes,
quality failures, and counterexamples without retuning the 76 H-alpha or 73 transfer galaxies.
Advance the ordered roadmap to Item 37, alternative geometry, on fresh data. Require explicit
torsion/nonmetricity/Finsler/affine equivalence boundaries, a GR and local limit, action and
stability proxies, and a response-blind observable that differs from an ordinary radial force
rewrite before response access.
