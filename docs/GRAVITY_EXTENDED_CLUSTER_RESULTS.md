# Extended length law: cluster development results

The 63-card transfer is complete across all eight registered development
clusters and 25 scenarios, with both comparators retained. It records 13,000
model/cluster/scenario predictions or retained failures. All 18 repeated card
records and both comparator records exactly reproduce the previous campaign.
Eleven prerequisite theory tests pass, and all frozen input snapshots verify.

Of the 45 new 100-pc--1-Mpc cards, 36 have scores under the existing numerical
rules. All nine 1-Mpc cards are withheld: their failures contain finite,
nonpositive inward-force samples. These are explicit force-validity failures,
not silently converted to pressure scores. Their source and numerical context
must be diagnosed before any broader physical exclusion.

## Pressure comparisons

Twenty of the 36 scored new cards have lower nominal descriptive pressure loss
than the empirical RAR comparator. None improves on its own zero-length
counterpart at the same shape and acceleration constant. Thus this extension
does not improve the nominal cluster loss over the matching scalar limit,
although it changes compatibility with the conditional local screens.

The lowest-loss new card is m=2, a0=2e-10 m/s^2, ell=100 pc. Its equal-cluster
mean squared log10 pressure-ratio loss is 0.003734806, versus 0.019777642 for
RAR. It has lower nominal loss in all eight clusters; that descriptive
advantage survives the recorded influence and trim summaries. Its median of
cluster median predicted/measured pressure ratios is 0.8814, so that summary
still indicates approximately 12 percent low pressure. Conditional whitened
mean squared residuals range from 2.13 to 4.57 across the three inherited
covariance treatments. These are not calibrated goodness-of-fit probabilities.

The comparison uses previously exposed development data. Wider source
reconstructions remain flagged where they exceed the primary source-fidelity
limit; nuisance sensitivities are not fitted corrections. Joint source/response
covariance, triaxiality, nonthermal pressure and outer source closure remain
limitations. No full Solar System, galaxy, lensing or independent-confirmation
claim follows from these pressure results.
