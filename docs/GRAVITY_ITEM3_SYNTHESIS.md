# Gravity roadmap Item 3 synthesis

## Scoped decision

**`REJECT_ITEM3_TESTED_LOCAL_SURFACE_VOLUME_DENSITY_FAMILIES_ADVANCE_ITEM4`**

Item 3 is complete for the tested local density families.  This does not say that every
density-dependent gravity law is false.  It says that two frozen attempts now cover both
coarse object-level summaries and smooth direct radial profiles, and neither produces a
universal cause that survives within-population, baseline, label, permutation, quality,
and robustness controls.

The deterministic synthesis receipt is
`runs/gravity/roadmap/item-03-synthesis-v1.json`.

## Evidence combined

Attempt 1 used equivalent-mass galaxy/cluster profiles plus 120 entirely fresh optical
groups.  The dual-transition variables failed within galaxies and clusters; on the 87
quality-valid groups, adding density lowered held-out `R^2` from 0.457 to 0.398 with
`p=0.795`.  Its representation also failed for 11 cross-scale objects and 33 groups, so
that attempt alone remained inconclusive.

Attempt 2 repaired the main representation concern with official smooth galaxy
surface-density profiles and X-COP gas-volume-density profiles.  The quality gate passes
for ten galaxies and all eight exploration clusters.  Yet every fold selects the
forbidden galaxy/cluster label.  The selector restricted to qualifying density formulas
has `R^2=-3.178` within galaxies and `R^2=-0.656` within clusters, loses to the
baryonic-acceleration baseline in both, has permutation `p=0.696`, and remains worse when
measured cluster stellar mass is added.

Together these results reject the frozen local amplitude, local scale-contrast,
dual-transition, and crossover families as a sufficient common cause.

## Failure-space boundary

The following regions should be recognized and skipped as equivalents in later searches:

- `u_surface/u_volume=3/D_M` is an Item 1 mass-dimension rewrite.
- `log(u_surface/u_volume)=log(3H/(2r))` in the smooth conversion is a local profile-scale
  rewrite.
- A binary galaxy/cluster indicator is a strong population proxy, not a causal variable.
- Retuning the same local density interactions on these opened responses is not a new
  attempt.

This closure does not cover nonlocal interior/exterior kernels, baryonic edges and shells,
environmental density, or density-dependent action/field-equation operators.  Those are
materially different roadmap items.
