are `0.5, 0.9, 0.5`. This is family-level coherence, not one exact universal parameter cell.

## Interpretation and failure space

The positive descriptive fact is narrow: in this 18-lens exploration set, a saturating nonlinear
enhancement near the familiar weak-acceleration scale organizes lensing residuals better than the
three frozen baryonic calibrations, especially for lower-mass and smaller lenses. The negative
fact is decisive for promotion: a search of this size finds comparable or stronger patterns in
half of the guarded null experiments, and stellar dynamics receives almost no incremental benefit
over the ordinary flexible model.

This result therefore does not establish self-interacting gravity, a universal galaxy/lens law,
historical novelty, direct image-level lensing success, or an alternative to dark matter or GR.
It preserves:

- the exact bounded-feedback family and its low-mass/small-size regime split as an unchanged future
  replication hypothesis;
- all four tested nonlinear regions and 27,242 physics-invalid cells as reusable failure-space and
  equivalence knowledge;
- the five confirmation lenses unopened.

The numbered roadmap advances to Item 30 screening mechanisms on fresh data. Item 30 must give
chameleon-, symmetron-, Vainshtein-like, and newly generated screening structures equal raw
capacity, freeze their environmental and local-limit predictions before responses, and must not
retune Item 29 on these 18 opened lenses.
