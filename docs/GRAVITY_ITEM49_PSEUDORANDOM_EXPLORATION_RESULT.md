# Gravity Roadmap Item 49: Pseudorandom Exploration Result

## Result in one sentence

The seeded pseudorandom search was a useful **discovery-engine PASS** because it found a
much better formula than an equal-budget sequential scan, but its best formula was a
**nonpromoted gravity result** because it predicted the joint galaxy-and-cluster data
worse than the existing Item 45 interaction model.

Decision: `NONPROMOTED_ITEM49_PSEUDORANDOM_RESULT_RETAINED`.

This is a retrospective result on already exposed data. It is not fresh confirmation, a
replacement for general relativity, evidence that dark matter has been eliminated, or a
claim that the selected expression is historically new.

## What was frozen before outcome scoring

- One grammar with 440 response-blind primitives: 64 Item 45 interaction coordinates,
  184 Item 46 dimensionless coordinates, 96 Item 47 operator coordinates, and 96 Item 48
  action coordinates.
- Eight unary transformations, eight binary operators, 16 mixing values, and three
  16-value outer parameter grids.
- A full addressable space of 6,496,138,035,200 ordinals.
- A collision-free seeded pseudorandom sample of 1,048,576 ordinals.
- An equal-budget sequential control of 1,048,576 ordinals starting at zero.
- Physical admissibility, symbolic-equivalence, and 112-row predictor-behavior-equivalence
  rules that use no observed outcome values.
- Five whole-object outer folds, four fixed baryonic-mass shifts, prior controls, and the
  global rule that one empirical counterexample—or a count of them—cannot kill a formula
  family.

The candidate and equivalence freeze read zero response fields. The seven Item 43
confirmation lenses remained sealed. There were no paid model calls and no candidates
were added after outcome evaluation.

## Selected pseudorandom program

All five outer folds and the full-data selection chose the same ordinal,
`1575724121867`, with this program coordinate:

`weighted_difference(tanh_2x(a0/(c*H*q**2)), 4*sin_pi_x(screened_auxiliary:bounded_log_acceleration:0.6))`

Its outer multiplier was

`nu = 1 + 6 u^(-0.2)/(1 + u/50) (0.05 + 0.95 H_program)`.

In plain language, the search combined a dimensionless cosmological/acceleration ratio
from Item 46 with a screened auxiliary-field coordinate from Item 48, transformed them
differently, subtracted the second from the first with weight four, bounded the result,
and used it to change the baryonic prediction most strongly at low acceleration.

The provenance label is `potentially_new_cross_mechanism_combination`. That means the
combination is new to this run's generated search history. Both ingredients and all
operators are known or previously generated, and no historical-novelty claim has been
made.

## Held-out result

Lower loss is better.

| Model | Balanced | S4TM galaxies | CLASH clusters |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Item 45 interaction control | 0.76148 | 0.18782 | 1.33514 |
| Item 47 operator control | 0.77891 | 0.16351 | 1.39431 |
| Pseudorandom Item 49 | 1.10118 | 0.17467 | 2.02769 |
| Item 48 action control | 1.81162 | 0.20481 | 3.41843 |
| Ordinary ridge | 1.87782 | 0.26047 | 3.49517 |
| Equal-budget sequential Item 49 | 3.93736 | 0.34068 | 7.53404 |

The pseudorandom search improved balanced loss by 72.03% relative to the equal-budget
sequential scan. This is the engine-level success: scattering samples across the grammar
reached materially better and more diverse behavior than reading one large contiguous
prefix.

The selected random formula nevertheless made balanced loss 44.61% worse than Item 45.
It slightly improved the S4TM galaxy component relative to Item 45, but its cluster error
was substantially larger. The paired sign-flip value was 0.0343; because the measured
direction favored Item 45, this is evidence of a disadvantage, not evidence for the new
formula.

All four fixed baryonic-mass variants remained worse than Item 45. There were 29 raw
object-level counterexamples relative to the strongest control and 20 that remained in
the same direction under every tested mass shift. They are retained as useful evidence,
but the data-quality policy does not permit global pruning: these are already exposed,
model-dependent data and there has been no unchanged independent replication.

## What materially improved

1. We can now map an integer to a formula deterministically across a multi-mechanism
   6.496-trillion grammar without storing the grammar.
2. We can sample more than one million unique locations without replacement and replay
   the exact sample from its seed.
3. We count raw ordinals, physical survivors, symbolic classes, behavioral classes, and
   actual outcome-scored work separately.
4. Predictor-only equivalence removes redundant behavior before outcomes are read.
5. Equal-budget sequential comparison demonstrates that search order matters rather than
   assuming randomness is creative.
6. The RTX 5090 performed all 71,429,680 candidate-point-fold evaluations, with the
   selected CPU/GPU loss agreeing within `2.7e-15`.
7. Every formula retains primitive provenance and an explicit rewrite/combination label.
8. Failures remain searchable evidence instead of becoming a one-counterexample veto.

## What did not improve

- Item 49 did not beat the best existing cross-scale formula.
- It did not solve the galaxy-cluster mismatch with one universal law.
- Its apparent structural stability is still stability inside one exposed data set; the
  same selected program in five folds is not five independent experiments.
- The action ingredient does not turn the sampled expression into a covariant theory.
- The current behavioral-equivalence classes are local to 112 predictor rows and can
  merge formulas that would differ on genuinely new systems.
- No causal variable, relativistic lensing derivation, conservation proof, stability
  proof, or strong-field completion follows from the fit.

## Scientific disposition

Retain the repeated cross-mechanism `weighted_difference` program as one diversity niche,
especially because it improves the galaxy component. Do not center the entire search on
it and do not prune the other mechanisms. Item 50 should let LLM ensembles propose
mechanisms and cross-domain syntheses under frozen provenance labels, then subject every
proposal to the same symbolic, dimensional, numerical, equivalence, and real-data checks.
