## Bottom line

The program has not discovered a viable replacement for general relativity and has not
shown that dark matter is unnecessary. It has produced one unusually interesting
phenomenological cluster result and then correctly exposed why that result is not yet a
theory.

The strongest surviving empirical lead is Item 59: a radial nonlocal/boundary response
predicted held-out X-COP pressure and temperature profile shapes on four frozen clusters,
conditional on one measured outer pressure anchor and the hydrostatic/nuisance model. Its
confirmation score improved by about 90% over every frozen comparator. Typical absolute
confirmation error remained about 19%.

The exact `beta=1.5` representation did not survive as a universal law:

- on 139 SPARC galaxies its equal-galaxy score was 625.4, versus 33.6 for the empirical
  RAR and 378.5 for Newtonian baryons;
- on 11 LITTLE THINGS galaxies it scored 71.0, versus 15.8 for RAR and 51.0 for
  Newtonian baryons;
- without a derived screen, its high-acceleration limit approaches 2.5 times ordinary
  gravity in the Solar System;
- it has no metric, photon coupling, conservation law, stability analysis, causal
  evolution, or cosmology.

These results veto universal promotion of the exact unscreened formula. They do not erase
the cluster fit or prune the broader nonlocal/boundary family.

The system now does more than fit a derived mass or one coefficient:

2. it freezes candidates and nuisance choices before opening reserved response rows;

The most important negative improvement is epistemic: the pipeline now catches a formula

## Counterexample policy

The machine-enforced policy is active in all new empirical gates:

- one empirical counterexample is retained and audited, never terminal;
- counterexample count alone is never sufficient;
- measurement uncertainty, calibration, covariance, distance, inclination, and reduction
  provenance must be considered where relevant;
- a finite empirical sample cannot prune an entire mechanism family;
- unchanged independent replication is required before an exact representation can be
  retired in a tested empirical scope.

A hard mathematical or physical witness is different from one imperfect data point. The
Solar-System result follows analytically from the exact formula's claimed universal
high-acceleration limit. It vetoes only that unscreened representation in that declared
domain; it does not veto every possible screened descendant.

## What remains interesting

The leading active mechanism is a **screened, transition-controlled descendant of the
nonlocal boundary response**. It needs two derived features:

- a measurable variable that suppresses the response in disks but preserves it in
  clusters, without using an object label;
- a universal high-acceleration screen that restores local gravity.

Compactness, baryonic boundary/profile state, geometry, thermodynamic state, and
environment remain candidate transition variables. Item 58 found some predictive signal
from cluster baryonic state, but it was model-dependent and did not survive every
uncertainty envelope. That is a lead, not a causal discovery.

Time variation, massive modes, QED-like running, resonance, memory, age, and alternative
geometry remain creative branches, but none currently has evidence comparable to Item 59
or a completed action-level theory. They are not killed; they are simply unpromoted.

## Independent confirmation and novelty

Every new internal replay is exact. External independent confirmation is still absent.
The four Item 59 confirmation clusters came from the same X-COP release and analysis
family, and no independent implementation has reproduced the result.

The exact radial occupancy/two-kernel expression was not located in the scoped prior-art
search and is behaviorally distinct from a purely local RAR rewrite. Its ingredients,
however, substantially overlap MOND/RAR, refracted gravity, nonlocal modified-Poisson
kernels, TeVeS, and recent source-kernel work. Its defensible label is “potentially new
synthesis of known motifs,” not historical novelty or new fundamental physics.
