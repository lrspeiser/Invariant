## Claim boundary and next action

This experiment does not rule out modified inertia generally. It tests bounded snapshot
projections of four action/control families over two resolved annuli, not full trajectories,
vertical motions, causal histories, a covariant action, lensing, or relativistic conservation.
The failure-space conclusion is narrower: on this fresh MaNGA problem, the search chooses only an
algebraic circular rewrite; it adds no noncircular prediction, loses badly to the strongest
structural baseline, is statistically unguarded, and depends on one favorable galaxy.

Preserve all 180,940 behavioral classes, the spectral/history equivalence, every counterexample,
and the exact `11761-12705` sensitivity without retuning the 119 H-alpha or 111 stellar galaxies
or opening the 40 confirmations. Advance the ordered roadmap to Item 36, extra dimensions, on
fresh data. Require an explicit four-dimensional limit, local/strong-field recovery, action and
stability checks, and an observable distinction from ordinary scale-dependent force rewrites
before response access.
