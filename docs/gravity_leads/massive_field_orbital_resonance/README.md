## Core idea

A massive field has a characteristic length lambda and frequency c/lambda. The search
asked whether an extra force turns on when a baryon-predicted orbital clock approaches
that carrier clock.

    Omega_b = sqrt(v_bar squared) / R

    z = n Omega_b lambda / c

    v_pred squared = v_bar squared + s A P(z) v_Y squared

P(z) was drawn from damped resonance, Landau-window, and two-harmonic coherence
families. The measured linewidth never entered the trigger.

## What was learned

- Three hundred fresh SFI++ exploration galaxies passed quality; 100 confirmations
  remained sealed.
- The generator tested 262,144 raw programs and 15.7 billion null-inclusive residuals.
- The candidate improved over fixed baryonic GR by 85.86%, calibrated baryonic GR by
  38.87%, and baryonic Tully-Fisher by 23.73%.
- It was 0.52% worse than a flexible seven-variable ordinary regression.
- The search-aware permutation result was p = 0.015.
- Every fold chose an enhancing force; four of five chose two-harmonic coherence.
- Four selected carrier ranges clustered within 0.75 dex.
- The highest-frequency stratum regressed, and comparisons with the flexible model
  split by mass and frequency.
- The current test used a global linewidth, not a resolved rotation curve.

## Relationship to known work

Massive scalar/vector carriers, Yukawa forces, running couplings, and oscillating fields
are known. Moffat's scalar-tensor-vector theory is an important comparator:
https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0506021

The exact two-harmonic orbital-clock coupling is labeled a potentially new synthesis of
known components, not established novelty.

## First-principles gap

A genuine resonance requires a time-dependent field equation, a physical driving term,
damping, energy accounting, and a stability analysis. The current formula is a static
force window shaped like resonance. It does not prove that a field was driven or that
galaxies synchronized over time.

4. Freeze the selected family and test a resolved rotation observable from a different
   survey.
5. Predict a phase-specific signature not available to ordinary structural regression.
