## Core idea

Gravity may be a macroscopic elastic or thermodynamic response of microscopic spacetime
information. The selected cell extended a known Verlinde-like radial response with an
acceleration transition:

    u = g_bar/a0

    g_pred/g_bar = 1 + 2.75 u^(-0.45) [1 + (u/0.01)^(0.5)]^(-1)

## What was learned

- The search gave equal raw capacity to four emergent/entropy/information niches.
- Of 262,144 cells, 128,411 passed response-independent physics gates and collapsed to
  122,106 behavioral classes.
- On three fresh KiDS mass-bin profiles, the selected formula beat a cross-fitted
  flexible ordinary spline by 14.09%.
- All three profiles improved, leave-one-profile-out did not reverse the result, and a
  frozen trim retained a 10.09% gain.
- With only three profiles, the exact sign-permutation result was p = 0.222, the smallest
  attainable value under the frozen test.
- Unchanged transfer to two color profiles was 1.67% worse than flexible.
- Unchanged transfer to an exposed model-dependent CLASH diagnostic was 55.67% worse
  than fixed MOND.
- The sealed fourth mass profile remains untouched.

## First-principles gap

Fitting an effective susceptibility does not derive microscopic degrees of freedom,
entropy counting, locality, causal dynamics, conservation, or a unique lensing rule.
The transition is phenomenological.
