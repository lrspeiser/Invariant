# Item 40: discrete/network gravity result

## Bottom line

Item 40 completed a real, response-blind search of graph-based gravity corrections. It generated
262,144 formulas in four equally sized graph niches, admitted 180,436 after frozen physical checks,
and evaluated 31,034,992 candidate-point combinations on an RTX 5090. The selected formula was then
transferred unchanged to 20 CLASH galaxy clusters.

The result is useful but not a new gravity theory. The graph formula beat gravity from catalogued
baryons alone and narrowly beat an equally trained ordinary-geometry model on the fresh WALLABY
galaxies. It did not beat MOND or Item 39's earlier formula, and it did not beat MOND on clusters.
The exact formula is therefore not promoted. The broader graph/network family is retained.

## What was tested

Each galaxy's response-blind baryonic radial profile became a network:

- radial mass annuli were graph nodes;
- adjacent annuli were connected by mass- and spacing-dependent conductances;
- a separate nonlocal graph connected every pair with a distance-decaying kernel;
- spectral position, effective resistance, heat-kernel return probability, and nonlocal
  communicability supplied four different formula niches;
- every graph feature used baryonic mass and geometry only, never observed rotation speed.

The selected candidate came from the nonlocal communicability niche:

`u = gbar/a0`

`nu = 1 + 4 u^-0.4 [1 + (u/1000)^0.2]^-5 [0.05 + 0.95 H_communicability^0.2]`

For galaxies, `v_pred = sqrt(nu) v_bar`. For the disclosed weak-field light proxy, the same
unchanged multiplier gives `g_lens,pred = nu gbar` with `Phi = Psi`. This is a phenomenological
closure, not a covariant action or complete theory.

## Fresh galaxy result

The predictor-only source left 60 WALLABY profiles unused by Item 39. Fourteen also passed the
already frozen stellar-mass, size, and graph-quality rules. Eleven were committed as exploration
objects and three as sealed confirmation objects before any Item 40 rotation response was opened.
Eight of the eleven exploration galaxies passed the response-quality rules, contributing 43 radial
points. The three failures remain recorded and were not replaced.

Equal-galaxy standardized held-out losses were:

| Model | Loss | Reading |
|---|---:|---|
| Item 39 frozen formula | 3.1823 | best tested baseline |
| MOND RAR | 3.8809 | second-best baseline |
| Item 40 graph candidate | 6.0338 | worse than Item 39 and MOND |
| Matched ordinary geometry | 6.0531 | graph candidate is only about 0.32% better |
| Flexible radial/surface ridge | 11.5207 | graph candidate is better |
| Baryonic Newton | 108.4211 | graph candidate is much better |

Seven of eight galaxies were raw worse-than-best-baseline counterexamples. Only two remained
uncertainty-resolved under the frozen-style paired point-loss audit. Removing the most influential
galaxy did not reverse the negative aggregate conclusion.

## Unchanged CLASH cluster diagnostic

After the galaxy-selected formula was committed, it was applied without retuning to 20 CLASH
clusters and 84 published radial acceleration points. Enclosed baryonic mass was reconstructed up
to a cancelling constant as `Mbar(<r) proportional to gbar r^2`, and the identical graph rule and
formula were used.

| Model | Equal-cluster loss |
|---|---:|
| MOND RAR | 41.2093 |
| Item 40 graph candidate | 69.8986 |
| Baryonic Newton | 134.3901 |

The graph candidate again improved greatly over baryonic Newton but was about 69.6% worse than
MOND. All 20 clusters were raw counterexamples relative to MOND; none was individually
uncertainty-resolved by the conservative within-cluster rule because each cluster has only three to
five radial points. This source was already exposed and is model-dependent, so it is a diagnostic,
not fresh confirmation or a direct image-likelihood lensing test.

## Why the formula is retained rather than killed

Astronomical catalogues have measurement, distance, inclination, calibration, stellar-mass, and
model-dependence uncertainties. The executable policy therefore does not let one empirical
counterexample—or a count by itself—kill a formula. Item 40 preserves every mismatch, distinguishes
raw from uncertainty-resolved failures, checks influence, and requires an unchanged independent
replication before even the exact representation can be retired in a tested domain. A finite data
set never prunes the entire network-gravity family.

Here the evidence is still clearly negative enough to withhold promotion. Retention means keeping
the formula and its failure map available for future independent tests or principled hybrids; it
does not mean calling the formula successful.

## Claim limits

- No dark-matter explanation was excluded.
- No modification of gravity was established.
- No covariant theory or continuum field equation was derived.
- Historical novelty was not established; graph, lattice, resistance, heat-kernel, and
  communicability ideas all have known prior art.
- The three confirmation galaxies remain sealed.
- No paid model calls were made.
