# Gravity roadmap Item 7 synthesis

## Decision

`REJECT_ITEM7_TESTED_GLOBAL_PHASE_COMPOSITION_GENERALIZATION_ADVANCE_ITEM8`

Item 7 is complete as a scoped test of the exact global stellar/atomic/molecular phase
families evaluated in two frozen real-data attempts. This is not a rejection of every role
for baryonic composition, nor evidence for or against general relativity or dark matter.

## What the two attempts established

### Attempt 1: resolved PHANGS CO rotation

The 31 quality-passing PHANGS galaxies produced a meaningful positive lead. Nonlinear phase
balance improved held-out MSE by `17.55%`, reached `R2=0.745`, helped every prespecified
slice, and entered four of five unrestricted folds. It was correctly not promoted: two
frozen quality failures remained, and the permutation result was `p=0.11`. Twelve
confirmations stayed sealed.

### Attempt 2: independent xCOLD GASS/xGASS replay

The exact prior interaction family was transferred without target-driven rewriting to an
independent whole-galaxy H I-width response. Ninety-five of 96 exploration galaxies passed
quality; the lone failure was a published zero uncertainty. On those 95, the replayed family
was `23.23%` worse than the strongest known baseline, lost in every prespecified stratum and
uncertainty envelope, entered zero unrestricted folds, and had permutation `p=0.882`.
Thirty-three xGASS and all twelve PHANGS confirmations stayed sealed.

## Failure-space knowledge retained

- Fixed baryonic Tully-Fisher, Newtonian mass/size, and raw phase main effects remain known
  or nonqualifying regions, not creative discoveries.
- The exact global phase entropy, stellar-gas boundary, atomic-molecular boundary,
  phase-ratio curvature, and composition-by-structure interactions cannot be promoted from
  either opened response set.
- The PHANGS pattern is preserved with the label `FAILED_INDEPENDENT_REPLAY_LEAD`; it is
  searchable counterexample knowledge, not erased evidence.
- H I mass and H I width share a spectrum in the replay, so instrument-independent causal
  composition was not established even before the predictive failure.
- Identity, per-galaxy coefficients, dynamical/dark mass, line width as a predictor, and
  lensing mass remain forbidden circular shortcuts.

## What remains open

Resolved multi-phase spatial geometry, ionized/plasma mass, action-derived composition
couplings, and materially different independent observables remain legitimate future ideas.
They must enter as new preregistered theory or data lanes, not as retuning of the opened
PHANGS or xGASS responses.

The next distinct first-principles variable family is Item 8: acceleration gradients,
baryonic-potential curvature, tidal invariants, and higher derivatives. It directly begins
testing spatial field structure that the global Item 7 proxies could not represent.
