## Equal-viability and two-track discovery policy

The numbered order is an execution and leakage-control order, **not a ranking of which idea is
allowed to be true**. Every mechanism begins as an equally viable hypothesis and receives the same
rules for target blindness, baselines, held-out evaluation, counterexamples, and provenance. A
familiar mechanism is not favored for being conventional, and a speculative mechanism is not
discarded for failing to explain the whole dark-matter problem in one test.

Every result is judged on two independent tracks:

1. **Universal-gravity track.** Does one derived law and universal parameter set survive the later
   galaxy, cluster, lensing, local-gravity, stability, causality, and cosmology gates?
2. **Phenomenon/publication track.** Does the frozen hypothesis discover a reproducible relation,
   variable, boundary, or counterexample on its actual domain, beyond strong ordinary baselines?
   A result may advance here even when it cannot yet, or cannot ever, be a complete gravity theory.

A branch is therefore never pruned solely because it is partial. A positive held-out result is
retained with its exact formula and scope; it earns a fresh-data replication or independent
confirmation test before a paper-level claim. A negative result closes only the tested
representation and remains useful as a publishable failure-space result when its exclusions are
mathematically or empirically broad. Evidence can change a branch's status, but not retroactively
change its starting priority or erase a distinct mechanism niche.

The Item 12 spectral-clock result is an active phenomenon/publication lead. Item 13 reproduced its
fixed consolidation on disjoint MaNGA identities after controlling for visible disturbance. Its
next paper-track test is an unchanged, cross-source confirmation of the age/surface-density term
with a fresh dynamical observable; it does not need to explain outer rotation curves or lensing to
be scientifically valuable. Those remain separate universal-gravity tests.
