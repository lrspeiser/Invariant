# A Range–Source Tradeoff for a Kinetically Gated Scalar Mediator

Status: narrow theory-note candidate. The theorem is exact only on the declared frozen-background asymptotic slice. Historical novelty, full model health, and observational success are not established.

## Abstract

Consider a two-scalar effective theory in which one scalar's kinetic invariant, (X), controls the mass of a second mediator, χ,

\[
\mathcal L=P(X)-V+Y_0X_\chi-\frac{m_\chi^2}{2}Z(X)\chi^2,
\qquad Y_0>0.
\]

Suppose (Z(X)\sim zX^s), with (z>0) and (s>1/2), while the positive margins entering the two frozen-background scalar sign conditions approach finite nonzero constants. We show that the allowed mediator amplitude, bare/frozen-φ range, and sufficient homogeneous source ceiling obey

\[
|\chi|_{\max}\sim X^{-(s-1)/2},\qquad
\ell_\chi\sim X^{-s/2},\qquad
Q_{\max}\sim X^{(s+1)/2}.
\]

Their product has the architecture-class leading law

\[
Q_{\max}\ell_\chi\sim \mathcal C(s,A_\infty,B_\infty,Y_0)\sqrt X,
\]

where the leading mediator mass (m_\chi), gate amplitude (z), and gate-power contribution to the (X) exponent cancel. Consequently, tolerating a source (Q\sim X^r) requires (s>2r-1), which forces the bare range to decrease faster than (X^{-(r-1/2)}). Within this restricted architecture there is no asymptotic choice that increases source capacity without paying a range penalty.

## 1. Assumptions and definitions

This note studies the committed split-gate action only on a constant-coefficient, fixed-background slice. Define

\[
C=P_X-QZ_X,\qquad
K=P_X+2XP_{XX}-Q(Z_X+2XZ_{XX}),\qquad
Q=\frac{m_\chi^2\chi^2}{2}.
\]

Let the positive background margins in the absence of the χ-amplitude correction approach (A_\infty>0) and (B_\infty>0). The local necessary conditions (C>0) and (K>0) then give sufficient amplitude ceilings

\[
\chi_C^2<\frac{2A_\infty}{m_\chi^2Z_X},\qquad
\chi_K^2<\frac{2B_\infty}{m_\chi^2(Z_X+2XZ_{XX})}.
\]

The bare/frozen-δφ mediator range is

\[
\ell_\chi=\frac{\sqrt{Y_0}}{m_\chi\sqrt Z}.
\]

It is not the general coupled pole when first-derivative φ–χ mixing is active.

## 2. Exact asymptotic derivation

For (Z\sim zX^s),

\[
Z_X\sim szX^{s-1},\qquad
Z_X+2XZ_{XX}\sim s(2s-1)zX^{s-1}.
\]

The second coefficient is positive when (s>1/2). Both amplitude bounds therefore scale as (X^{-(s-1)/2}). The mediator range follows immediately as (X^{-s/2}).

For the frozen constant-coefficient Yukawa problem, a sufficient source ceiling is

\[
Q_{\max}=m_\chi^2 Z\chi_{\max}.
\]

Hence (Q_{\max}\sim X^{(s+1)/2}). Branch by branch,

\[
Q_{\max}\ell_\chi\sim
\sqrt{Y_0}\min\left[
\sqrt{\frac{2A_\infty}{s}},
\sqrt{\frac{2B_\infty}{s(2s-1)}}
\right]\sqrt X.
\]

The leading (m_\chi) and (z) factors cancel. This cancellation is universal only within the declared architecture and asymptotic assumptions; it is not a universal law of gravity.

## 3. Critical source growth and the no-free-range corollary

If a homogeneous or long-wave source grows as |(Q_\chi)| (\sim X^r), then

\[
\frac{|Q_\chi|}{Q_{\max}}\sim X^{r-(s+1)/2}.
\]

Thus (r<(s+1)/2) is asymptotically subcritical, equality is coefficient-dependent, and (r>(s+1)/2) is supercritical. Strict subcriticality requires

\[
s>2r-1.
\]

But ℓχ scales as (X^{-s/2}), so the same inequality implies that the range must shrink faster than (X^{-(r-1/2)}). Stronger source tolerance is purchased with stronger range suppression.

For the illustrative family (Z=(1+\beta X^2)^p), (s=2p), giving

\[
\ell_\chi\sim X^{-p},\quad
\chi_{\max}\sim X^{1/2-p},\quad
Q_{\max}\sim X^{p+1/2}.
\]

The committed (p=2) result, ℓχ ∼ (X^{-2}), χmax ∼ (X^{-3/2}), and (Q_{\max}\sim X^{5/2}), is recovered exactly.

## 4. Relation to published screening mechanisms

The theorem is adjacent to, but not presently identified as an exact rewrite of:

- [chameleon screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0309300), where the mediator mass depends on matter density;
- [symmetron screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.4525), where density restores a symmetry and suppresses the coupling;
- [k-mouflage](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.2943) and the [kinetic two-body problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07725), where nonlinear self-kinetics screen the fifth force;
- [general Horndeski fifth-force screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6401), which supplies broader nonlinear source context;
- [Goldstone models of modified gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08279), which include kinetically dependent conformal couplings;
- [multi-field dilaton screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13986), which uses a field-dependent kinetic metric; and
- [technically natural fifth-force suppression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20292), which derives a symmetry-controlled mass–strength correlation.

A bounded targeted search found no explicit derivative-invariant (Z(X)\chi^2) range/source exponent theorem or the product law above. That is evidence for plausible originality, not proof of historical priority.

## 5. What would falsify or demote the result

The note should be demoted if any of the following occurs:

1. An algebraically equivalent published theorem is identified.
2. Independent derivation finds that the assumed asymptotic margins cannot remain positive in the action.
3. The full coupled pole reverses the bare-range interpretation in every admissible background.
4. Radiative corrections make the derivative-dependent mass gate technically inconsistent below the needed cutoff.
5. No on-shell material source can realize the assumed (X) and (Q_\chi) scaling.

## 6. Claims not made

This note does not prove a healthy modified-gravity model, causal completeness, nonlinear screening of a material body, Solar-System viability, a galaxy or cluster prediction, observational support, historical novelty, or publication readiness. It identifies a compact analytic constraint that may be worth independent expert and priority review.
