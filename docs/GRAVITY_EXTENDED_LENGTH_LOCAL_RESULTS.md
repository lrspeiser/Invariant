# Extending the physical length search beyond 10 parsecs

All 45 newly tested global parameter cards, spanning 100 pc to 1 Mpc, fall
within the existing conditional local summary screens. All 18 repeated zero-
and 10-pc controls exactly reproduce their earlier stored result values.
The 63-card run has 57 within-screen results and six outside-screen results;
the six outside cases are repeated zero-length controls, not new exclusions.
Seventeen independent theory-control tests pass before the scan.

## Why this extension matters

The original 0--10 pc grid was explicitly chosen to probe compact-system
screening below disk thickness scales. It was never a physical upper bound on
the action's length parameter. The completed small-length calculations predict
very small galaxy changes, so stopping at that grid would leave physically
different disk- and cluster-scale responses untested.

Before evaluating the new predictions, the same three shapes and three global
acceleration constants were combined with fixed lengths 100, 1,000, 10,000,
100,000 and 1,000,000 pc. Zero and 10 pc were retained as repeated controls.
No length was fitted per object or selected from the new local results. The
original 54-card campaigns and all numerical failures remain intact.

## What was checked, and what remains open

Each card retains all six inherited monopole-orbit summaries and both declared
external-field backgrounds for the quadrupole summary. The existing quadrature,
independent action/flux, regulator-sensitivity and perturbative-validity checks
are unchanged. These are previously exposed historical summaries for an
idealized point source plus constant external field. Passing them does not
establish a full Solar System fit or a relativistic light prescription.

The new range is therefore eligible for further development tests, not admitted
as a successful theory. Compute its galaxy and cluster responses with the same
global cards, retain numerical failures and nonpositive branches, and compare
both dynamics and lensing only with adequate source and measurement models.
The current direct small-signal refinement campaign continues unchanged.
Conservation, stability, physical source uncertainty, direct outer-star data
and independent cross-regime validation remain requirements of the full goal.
