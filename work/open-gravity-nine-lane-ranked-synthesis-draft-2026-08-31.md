“Promise” below means closeness to a decisive, interpretable real-data test. It is not a probability that the idea is true.

## Strongest results

### Strongest observation

Galileo E14 is the strongest observation because it is an actual registered, held-out real-response test with a frozen phase control. Its small nominal improvement is overwhelmed by a deliberately wrong registered phase. The honest conclusion is rejection of that specific registered phase law, not of all memory mechanisms.

### Strongest theorem

A stationary change in propagation speed cannot by itself strengthen a static gravitational field: changing travel time changes when influence arrives, but a time-independent source yields the same stationary field unless the theory adds attenuation, memory, a modified Green function, extra state, or different source coupling. This sharply narrows Lane 2 without ruling out its richer branches.

A secondary exact result is the GQNS monotonicity theorem: for fixed positive `A_Q` and finite `L`, the source-only enhancement increases with radius, so one common inverse-square rescaling cannot match two unequal radii.

### Strongest method

The strongest reusable product is the response-blind gate system: immutable source hashes, exploration/confirmation roles, target-free operator tests, planted-signal recovery, conventional countermodels, independent executor audits, and retained failures. Lane 5 stopping before strain and Lane 9 consuming single-use authorizations, retaining both parser- and null-contract failures, and producing zero score are concrete demonstrations. This method is more publication-ready than any positive new-gravity claim.
