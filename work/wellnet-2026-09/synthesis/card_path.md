# Principle card: reciprocal path-dependent gravity

`P_reciprocal_path` -- a reciprocal two-point kernel gated on the vacuum fraction of the connecting segment, with the matter on the segment as the declared momentum carrier.

## 1. Physical statement -- what does nature do differently?

The gravitational interaction between two mass elements depends on the matter/vacuum state of the straight segment joining them: the pair kernel is -(G/d)[1 + eps v], with v the fraction of the segment along which the baryonic density lies below a universal density rho_*. Gravity is transmitted differently through emptiness than through matter (eps > 0: more strongly through vacuum). Every pair relation is reciprocal by construction, and the momentum a pair does not conserve is carried by the matter on its segment, which feels the gradient of a three-body potential Phi_3 that is an explicit function of the local density and of the product of the two opposite half-columns through the point.

## 2. Source -- which observable physical quantity creates it?

Rest mass rho, in two roles: as the endpoints of every pair (the rho(x) rho(y) weight) and as the STATE along the path (phi(rho) = 1/(1 + rho/rho_*) integrated along the segment). No other source, no preferred axis. The state is explicit, gauge-free (density, not potential), representation-independent (a continuum functional), and measurable: the baryonic column along the segment is what X-ray, HI and absorption observations return.

## 3. State -- what carries it?

A nonlocal scalar relation W(x,y)[rho]. What a test body or photon sees is nevertheless ONE scalar potential, Phi = delta E/delta rho = Phi_dir + Phi_3, with Phi_3(z) = -(G eps/2) phi'(rho(z)) P(z) and P(z) = Int dOmega C(z,n) C(z,-n), the angular integral of the product of the two opposite half-line columns through z -- derived by collapsing the double integral over pairs whose segment passes through z. Phi_3 is non-zero only where matter lies on BOTH sides of z: inside a body, or in the bridge between two bodies. No vector field, no memory.

## 4. Propagation

Path-dependent (the straight segment between the two points), instantaneous weak-field response: the functional is static. Not retarded, not history-dependent. Finite propagation would need a relativistic completion the static action does not supply (assumption, flagged).

## 5. Matter coupling -- how it alters massive-particle motion

a = -grad Phi, universal. Measured on the compiler's own probe caricatures at the fiducial (eps = 0.3, rho_* = 1e-24): (i) ENDPOINT TERM: for eps > 0 the force is WEAKER in the shell where the vacuum fraction rises with radius (the extra binding grows outward, so the force falls) and tends to G(1+eps) beyond: isolated galaxy g/g_N = 0.859 at its minimum near 28 kpc, 0.864 at 30 kpc; cluster 0.945 at 300 kpc, 0.892 at 1 Mpc, 1.171 at 2.8 Mpc -- the WRONG sign for the cluster excess inside 1.2 Mpc for eps > 0; (ii) CARRIER TERM: any dense body embedded in a medium near rho_* sits in a Phi_3 trough. A cluster member galaxy has its internal gravity multiplied by 7.56 at 20 kpc and 2.72 at 30 kpc, and the net force turns OUTWARD beyond ~38 kpc: at this amplitude the family fails the member-galaxy regularity that the tensor lane already identified as the binding constraint on anything that switches on inside cluster galaxies (everything is linear in eps, so eps <~ 0.003 keeps this at the 2% level); (iii) MEMBER-MEMBER PAIRS: the mass-weighted vacuum fraction of member-member segments is 0.333 against 0.347 +- 0.0036 under angle scrambles at fixed radii and masses -- the member-locked response is at the 0.11% level of the pair force: connectivity lives in the GAS columns and in the bridges, not in member positions; (iv) PAIR FORCE vs an intervening filament: log-slope 0.87 in the far endpoint mass (Newtonian filament pull: -0.00) and 0.87 -> 0.08 in the filament density, saturating (Newtonian: 1.00, never saturating).

## 6. Photon coupling -- lensing, time delay, frequency, polarization

Photons see the same Phi = Phi_dir + Phi_3 (common geometry, no slip). Between two mass concentrations Phi_3 is a ridge along the segment: for eps > 0 a potential HILL of 1.21e+06 (km/s)^2 at the midpoint of two 3e+14 Msun clusters 4 Mpc apart at the fiducial (linear in eps), whose effective density is negative on the axis (-8.7e-23 kg/m^3) and positive around it; its projected effective surface density is a COMPENSATED profile, -0.49 kg/m^2 on the axis and +0.16 kg/m^2 in the wings, and its volume integral vanishes (net/|net| = 0.035 within the computed box, zero in the infinite volume by Gauss). The amplitude scales as M_A M_B (measured 2.001, 4.000 against 2, 4) and as -phi'(rho_bridge). No frequency or polarization dependence; a photon's deflection depends on its path only through where P is non-zero along it.

## 7. Known limits -- why Solar-System, high-acceleration and wave constraints recover

SOLAR SYSTEM, LABORATORY, PULSARS, WIDE BINARIES: exact Newtonian limit, not by absorbing a constant into G -- the interplanetary and interstellar media (~1e-21 to 1e-20 kg/m^3) are DENSER than rho_* for any rho_* below ~1e-22, so v = 0 on every such segment, and P at a planet is ~1e-13 kg^2/m^4, giving Phi_3 ~ 1e-8 m^2/s^2. GALAXY REGULARITIES: in-plane disk segments run through gas denser than rho_* (v ~ 0); the modification appears where the density falls below rho_* (~25 kpc for the caricature galaxy), as a 14% weakening of the force at the fiducial amplitude, then G(1+eps) beyond. The binding limit is the CLUSTER MEMBER: x7.6 at 20 kpc at eps = 0.3, which bounds eps/rho_* by member internal dynamics -- data in the confirmation reserve, not opened. WAVE PROPAGATION: the static functional says nothing about the tensor-wave sector; GW speed = c is an assumption on the completion (flagged).

## 8. Counterfactual signature

Rotate an external axis: nothing (no axis). Move the baryons holding a halo: the response follows the NEW segments' columns instantly (v, P re-evaluated). Move a halo holding baryons: nothing. Scramble members preserving every radial profile: the member-member vacuum fraction moves by 0.0036 (1 sd), i.e. the pair forces by 0.11% at eps = 0.3 -- tiny, and the sign of the change is that of the change in blocked path length. Change history preserving present matter: nothing. Change the photon path preserving endpoints: a path through the bridge between two concentrations crosses a compensated feature (dO/dB: negative core convergence for eps > 0), a path of equal length avoiding it sees none. Insert a filament between two concentrations at fixed endpoints: the pair force WEAKENS for eps > 0 (dF/F = -0.070 at rho_f = rho_*), with slope ~1 in the far endpoint mass and saturating in the filament density; the same filament's own Newtonian pull is independent of the far endpoint and linear in the density. Embed a dense body in a medium near rho_*: it is confined (eps > 0) or expelled (eps < 0), with the trough depth ~ (G eps/2 rho_*) P_medium.

## 9. Unique falsifier -- fails even after nuisances are marginalised

The bridge between pairs of mass concentrations, after the endpoints' own profiles are subtracted, must show a COMPENSATED feature -- zero net mass, negative core for eps > 0 -- in BOTH stacked convergence and the dynamics of bodies on the segment, with amplitude scaling as M_A M_B (slope 1 in each endpoint mass at fixed separation) and depending on the bridge's own baryonic column with the universal shape -phi'. A bridge with net positive mass; or a bridge signal that scales with the filament's own mass rather than with M_A M_B; or one that survives when one endpoint mass -> 0; or a cluster member whose internal dynamics show no trough at an amplitude where the bridge does -- each kills the law. Positivity of collisionless density (rho_DM >= 0) cannot produce a compensated feature, so no halo nuisance repairs it. NOT a falsifier: a monopole change of cluster gravity, or the smooth-gas part of the response, which is radial (BJ.1).

## 10. CDM distinction -- why a realistic collisionless halo cannot reproduce the JOINT response

CDM's bridge is real mass: rho_DM >= 0, net positive convergence, set by the local density field and only statistically tied to the endpoints; CDM member forces depend on the pair separation and on the local dark-plus-baryonic mass, never on what lies on the segment beyond that mass's own pull. The path law's JOINT response -- (i) a compensated, zero-net-mass bridge with a sign fixed by eps; (ii) amplitude proportional to M_A M_B times -phi'(rho_bridge); (iii) pair forces that respond to an intervening filament with slope 1 in the FAR endpoint mass and saturation in the filament density; (iv) one functional tying the bridge's lensing to the acceleration of bodies on it -- cannot be reproduced by any rho_DM >= 0: (i) is a positivity obstruction, not a fine-tuning; (ii)-(iv) would require the dark matter between every pair to be arranged in proportion to the product of the endpoint masses and to saturate with the gas density. The smooth-gas (radial) part of the response and any monopole rescaling of cluster gravity carry NO distinguishing power and are excluded from the claim.
