Abstract:
    Of the eROSITA/eFEDS sources (Brunner et al., 2022A&A...661A...1B,
    Cat. J/A+A/661/A1, Paper I), 24774 of 27369 have reliable counterparts
    (90.5%) in the main sample and 231 of 246 sources (93.9%) have
    counterparts in the hard sample, including 2514 (3) sources for which
    a second counterpart is equally likely. By means of reliable spectra,
    Gaia parallaxes, and/or multi-wavelength properties, we have
    classified the reliable counterparts in both samples into Galactic
    (2695) and extragalactic sources (22079). For about 340 of the
    extragalactic sources , we cannot rule out the possibility that they
    are unresolved clusters or belong to clusters. Inspection of the
    distributions of the X-ray sources in various optical/IR
    colour-magnitude spaces reveal a rich variety of diverse classes of
    objects. The photometric redshifts are most reliable within the
    KiDS/VIKING area, where deep near-infrared data are also available.
    Together with the catalogues of primary and secondary counterparts to
    the main and hard samples of the eFEDS survey, this paper releases
    their multi-wavelength properties and redshifts. The CTP2 catalog
    lists the possible additional counterparts for about 2500 sources in
    the Main catalog.

Description:
    We present the identification of the counterparts to the
    point sources in eFEDS listed in the main and hard catalogues (Brunner
    et al. (2022)), together with the study of their multiwavelength
    properties.

Global notes:
Note (G1): counterpart quality as follows:
            4 = best
            3 = good
            2 = with secondary
            1 = unreliable. (see flow chart paper)
            0 = unreliable. (see flow chart paper)
Note (G2): CTP classification as follows:
            3 = secure extragalactic
            2 = likely extragalactic
            1 = secure galactiC
            0 = likely galactic
Note (G3): Normalised quality of spectroscopic redshift as follows:
            3 = secure
            2 = not secure
            1 = unreliable
           -1 = Blazar candidate
Note (G4): Extension in HSC griz bands as follows:
             1 = extended
           -99 = data missing
             0 = other from Aihara et al., 2018PASJ...70S...4A
 (Expression: ($107==-99&&$108==-99&&$109==-99&&$110==-99)?-99:HSC_opt_extended)
Note (G5): see Appx. in Salvato et al 2018MNRAS.473.4937S for clarifications
Note (G6): Match to Hamstar as follows:
            1 = same counterpart
            0 = different counterpart
          -99 = no Hamstar (Schneider et al., 2022A&A...661A...6S)
