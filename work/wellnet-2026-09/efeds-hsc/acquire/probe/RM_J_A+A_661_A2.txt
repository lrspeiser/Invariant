Abstract:
    The eROSITA Final Equatorial-Depth Survey has been carried out during
    the PV phase of the SRG/eROSITA telescope and completed in November
    2019. In the 140 deg2 area covered by eFEDS, 542 candidate clusters
    and groups are detected as extended X-ray sources, down to a flux of
    ~10^-14^erg/s/cm2 in the soft band (0.5-2keV) within 1'. We provide
    the catalog of candidate galaxy clusters and groups in eFEDS. We
    perform imaging and spectral analysis on the eFEDS clusters with
    eROSITA X-ray data, and study the properties of the sample. The
    clusters are distributed in the redshift range [0.01, 1.3], with the
    median redshift at 0.35. We obtain the ICM temperature measurement
    with >2{sigma} c.l. for ~1/5 (102/542) of the sample. The average
    temperature of these clusters is ~2keV. Radial profiles of flux,
    luminosity, electron density, and gas mass are measured from the
    precise modeling of the imaging data. The selection function, the
    purity and completeness of the catalog are examined and discussed in
    detail. The contamination fraction is ~1/5 in this sample, dominated
    by misidentified point sources. The X-ray Luminosity Function of the
    clusters agrees well with the results obtained from other recent X-ray
    surveys. We also find 19 supercluster candidates in eFEDS.

Description:
    This catalog contains the 542 galaxy cluster and group candidates
    detected in the eROSITA Final Equatorial-Depth Survey (eFEDS). The
    coordinates, redshift, and main X-ray properties of these sources
    including temperature, luminosity, and flux, measured with the eROSITA
    data, are presented.
