# Frozen noise recipe transfers to a second galaxy

The NGC2976 recipe, with no new model selection, produces NGC3198 eastern-background joint q/N **1.0276**. The six aperture averages span **0.9482–1.0344**, and all 576 spatial-mode averages satisfy the predeclared descriptive 0.8–1.2 range. Aperture trace ratios span 0.9317–1.0530. This is encouraging transfer of a measurement-noise recipe, not evidence for a gravity theory or admission of a galaxy cube likelihood.

The low-spatial-frequency band's lowest channel-eigenvalue quartile is **1.2621**, outside the descriptive range; all other reported quartile and frequency-half aggregates lie inside it. Individual DC channels satisfy the range in 33 of 72 cases. With only 18 eastern cores, those individual channel flags are not statistical rejection: finite-sample variation, training uncertainty and unknown dependence matter. No p-values or independent-core assumption were used. None of 1,104 adjacent mode products exceeds absolute 0.1, which is not a proof that all modes or cores are independent. Every diagnostic is retained without retuning.

NGC3198 has 72 stored channels compared with NGC2976's 42. The preflight generalized covariance dimensions before cube-value access, retained every channel, and kept the four spatial bands, per-mode powers and shrinkages fixed. Only the western channel mean, mode powers and covariance amplitudes were refitted. The selected DC recipe remained 50% common-variance shrinkage times (n+1)/(n-1); no western or eastern selection was repeated.

The older broad NGC3198 annulus was not reused unchecked: its receipt showed 11.21% direct MOM0 overlap and 26.44% expanded overlap. The new frozen native support uses the entire released positive MOM0 footprint expanded by 120 arcsec, the 550–680 arcsec annulus, west/east +/-90 arcsec guard and 42-pixel image-edge exclusion. Exactly 20 western and 18 eastern disjoint 24x24 cores qualify on the fixed 48-pixel lattice. The selection never uses the truncated baryon map. NGC2903 fallback was not needed or opened. Same-observation MOM0 screening is source-dependent and cannot certify pure background; historical development exposure remains disclosed.

Pre-access controls passed at 7 and 72 channels: positive covariance with rank-deficient DC samples, unit scaling, Parseval, explicit dense inverse and aperture covariance. Independent replay reads back all 38 cores from the native cube, verifies their geometric/source-mask exclusion with a separate nearest-neighbor calculation, uses a manual cosine basis, reconstructs all four western covariance fits and checks every held core score, 576 mode scores, 26 group diagnostics, 72 channel scores and six aperture summaries. Maximum absolute discrepancy is 3.67e-13. Cross-mode products are retained production diagnostics; the independent receipt does not claim a separate replay of those products.

Source: [THINGS archive](https://things.www3.mpia.de/Data.html), [Walter et al. 2008 measurement paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.2125). Hashes bind the existing native cube, complete MOM0 map, original spectral receipt, support decisions, protocol and implementation dependencies. Zero new raw downloads, no source-region cube pixels or observed velocity targets, no reserved galaxy arrays. Production evaluation took 1.53 seconds on one CPU thread; private extracted cores remain outside publication. The likelihood remains blocked on source-region noise, response and mask validation, mean uncertainty, cross-mode/core dependence and more independent transfer evidence.
