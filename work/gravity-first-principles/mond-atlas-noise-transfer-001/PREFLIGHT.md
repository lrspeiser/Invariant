Scope: conditional background diagnostic, SOURCE_BLOCKED for emission or gravity likelihood. Read admission policy v1. No velocities or reserved galaxies. Source release: THINGS natural-weight cubes and MOM0, https://things.www3.mpia.de/Data.html; primary measurement paper Walter et al. 2008, https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.2125 (verified September 6, 2026). MOM0 is same-observation screening, not independent noise certification. The new spatial split is historically exposed development data.

Before cube-value access, assess NGC3198 then NGC2903 only, in that order, from native headers and complete released MOM0 footprints. Do not use tapered mass maps or response residuals. Match MOM0/cube spatial WCS. Retain finite positive MOM0 union nonfinite MOM0 as excluded, expand by 120 arcsec using exact Euclidean distance on native pixels. Require signed SIN plane annulus 550–680 arcsec about header reference center, west/east beyond +/-90 arcsec, and 42-pixel edge exclusion. Cores are 24 square on centers 24+48i; every core pixel must qualify, with at least 12 cores per half. If neither qualifies, stop before cube arrays. Do not relax masks. Preserve rejected candidates and counts. This is conservative screened background, not proven emission-free sky; source-dependent screening remains a limitation.

Recipe frozen from NGC2976: constant per-channel mean learned west; complete orthonormal 24x24 DCT; bands kx²+ky²=0,1..9,10..64,>64. Each positive mode power is its western mean square across cores and channels, floored at max(1e-300,1e-12 times band median positive raw power), normalized to band mean one. No pooling or exclusions. Per-band covariance of normalized coefficients uses western ML second moment, diagonal floor max(1e-12,1e-8 median variance), diagonal shrink DC=1, low=.6, middle=.3, high=.1. Replace DC diagonal with (n+1)/(n-1)*[.5*diagonal+.5*mean(diagonal)]. No selection, including western selection, in the new galaxy. Freeze fitted matrices before east access. All available channels retained; the known NGC3198 metadata has 72, so all occurrences of 42 generalize to channel count c, with four contiguous eigenvalue quartiles via array_split. No frequency resampling or bandpass equivalence claim.

Pre-access independent controls: explicit small dense covariance/inverse versus modal q, Parseval and aperture covariance, scaling with data units, dimension-generalized 7 and 72-channel positive covariance including rank-deficient DC. After transfer save every core q/logpdf, 576 modes, frequency halves and channel eigenvalue quartiles, individual DC channel q, adjacent mode products, and all six aperture q/trace ratios. Frozen descriptive q flags [.8,1.2]; no pixel-based significance. Independent arithmetic replay must reconstruct western moments and eastern scores. No eastern retuning after failures. Maximum five-minute numerical run, one CPU thread, no GPU, no downloads, public <20MB, private core arrays <100MB. Bind scripts, preflight, headers/source receipts and source file hashes.
