Frozen before tests: verify lapse/potential mapping by a fourth-order finite difference at dimensionless radii 0.5, 1, 2, 10, step 1e-4 r, relative error < 1e-8; Schwarzschild static-observer acceleration identity < 1e-12; static photon Killing-energy bookkeeping < 1e-14; two-reservoir exchange analytic energy sum < 1e-12; relaxation equilibrium independent of positive time constant < 1e-12; zero-frequency damped-oscillator response independent of damping and stable positive coefficients. These are manufactured/analytic tests, not a new 3D solver or observation.

The equations proposed here are our illustrative candidates, not claims that these papers establish a time-energy reservoir.
