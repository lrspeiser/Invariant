# Delayed relay benchmark: frozen before implementation

Disposition: THEORY_BENCHMARK_ONLY. No observational source or response data are opened. This is a dimensionless manufactured response system, not a fitted gravitational theory or a test of stellar age. Time is measured in an arbitrary unit; no physical value of tau is inferred.

Primary context: Chicone and Mashhoon, Nonlocal Gravity: Modified Poisson's Equation, https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.4702, introduces spatial nonlocal source terms and successive approximation. Our temporal equations below are our own toy hypothesis, not those authors' complete theory. Independent benchmarks are exact scalar-mode ODE solutions and independently diagonalized matrix exponentials. No claim of relativistic causality, conserved physical energy, or lensing is made.

Equations: tau dS/dt + S = K rho (single generation); tau dS/dt + S = K(rho + alpha S) (recursive relay). K is a symmetric, positive, circulant normalized Gaussian on a periodic 16-cell ring; sigma is 1, 2, or 4 cells. Its largest eigenvalue is 1. The manufactured boundary is periodic, not a galaxy boundary. Check normalization, symmetry and cyclic translation. Repeat sizes 16, 32, 64 at fixed physical ring circumference and fractional width 1/8; compare low Fourier eigenvalues as a discretization check. This ring cannot establish 3D boundary independence.

For an eigenmode lambda and step input b: S = lambda b [1-exp(-(1-alpha lambda)t/tau)]/(1-alpha lambda); at equality S=lambda b t/tau. Frequency transfer is lambda/(1-alpha lambda+i omega tau). Sweep alpha 0, .25, .5, .8, .9, .95, .99, 1, 1.01, 1.1; tau .1, 1, 10; omega .001, .01, .1, 1, 10. Preserve every result, including unstable cases. Numerical integration uses solve_ivp DOP853 rtol 1e-10 atol 1e-12; verify step and complex frequency against analytic solutions to relative/absolute 1e-8; matrix reference tolerance 1e-8. Refine integration tolerance for convergence. Stable feedback requires alpha < 1 for these nonnegative normalized kernels. Alpha=1 is marginal in the homogeneous equation and grows linearly under persistent input; alpha>1 grows exponentially.

Record static source enhancement 1/(1-alpha) for the uniform response, relaxation time tau/(1-alpha), response at age 10 tau, and frequency amplitude and phase. Static measurements alone cannot identify tau. A source enhancement is not automatically the same enhancement of acceleration at every point. The Gaussian integral is finite; the untruncated NFW response integral ln(1+x)-x/(1+x) diverges, precluding an automatic finite-budget normalized recursion without an outer modification.

No physical energy budget is proved by source normalization. Numerical stability is necessary, not evidence that the relay mechanism exists. All gates precede any observational scoring; such scoring is forbidden in this package.
