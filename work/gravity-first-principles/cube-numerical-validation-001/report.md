# Cube machinery milestone

CUDA PyTorch 2.7.1+cu128 on RTX 5090 passes gradient, held-out noiseless prediction and synthetic channel-noise whitening controls. Four known-source injections (rotation, warp, radial streaming and asymmetric line profiles) recover withheld spectra with their matching model; all 20 candidate optimizations converge. These are same-model computational controls, not independent physical validation.

The observation pilot is still a separate development experiment. No new gravity law is supported by this milestone.

Stellar/CO acquisition completed: 53 public assets, 249,250,218 bytes, zero download failures, with source URLs and SHA-256 hashes. Acquiring these maps does not establish total density; stellar population conversion, foreground contamination, molecular conversion and coverage remain to be audited.
