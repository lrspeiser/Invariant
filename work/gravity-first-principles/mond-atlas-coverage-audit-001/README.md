# Coverage audit: the program tested several proxies, not all proposed mechanisms

The user's concern is justified: the completed radial comparison does **not** establish that all competing ideas received equally thorough tests. Numerical correctness and careful train/test separation are strengths, but they do not make a narrow proxy a complete test of its parent mechanism. The clock branch received extra structural repairs that several contemporaneous branches did not. Ranking these tested formulas is useful; ranking the full underlying ideas would overstate the evidence.

This read-only audit reviews current relay/clock reports, their frozen grids, the earlier structure/environment/motion experiments, motion-control reports, the broad hypothesis inventory, and the project task handoff. It computes inventory counts only, with **no new response scoring**. Historical receipts indexed by older inventories are not independently revalidated here.

## Count formulas honestly

The initial 713 entries are parameter settings in 11 implementation labels. They represent **nine algebraic forms** if fixed/adjusted Newton are grouped together and fixed/adjusted MOND are grouped together. Seven forms are novel radial proxies; two are baseline laws. The mixture already contains the pure p2 and p3 endpoints, and zero-strength candidates duplicate Newton predictions.

The mass-scale repair evaluated 622 settings: 612 across five alternative source-scaling prescriptions and ten repeated MOND controls. The central repair evaluated 144 settings: one additional central-force shape under two scale prescriptions. Altogether this is **1479 evaluated settings**, not 1479 independent ideas. Counting mathematical force shapes under this explicit convention gives ten including Newton/MOND; counting a changed source-scaling prescription as a separate implementation gives more variants, but still far fewer than the grid size. Counts are descriptive, not a scientifically unique taxonomy.

Larger grids do not imply better tests of physical mechanisms. The latest clock family received four source/shape combinations (original, mass scaling, core taper, both). Kernel/p2/p3/mixture received two scale prescriptions each. Surface relay and absorption received one formulation each. Memory, actual distributed fields, velocity couplings and nested arrangements were not advanced to a new real-galaxy field test in this milestone.

## What robustness was and was not established

Established: correct units for tested formulas, analytic limits, manufactured numerical checks, independent replay, source hashes, restricted cohort, global training parameters, whole-galaxy selection and retained failed repairs. These substantially reduce implementation errors.

Not established: equal structural search effort; an unseen confirmation sample; exhaustive formula coverage; observational 3D geometry; full source uncertainty; full joint covariance; validity across environments/surveys; dynamics, energy transfer, stability or lensing for every branch. Small synthetic noise ensembles and descriptive bootstraps do not replace those checks. Several preferred parameter values touch grid edges, so even those limited forms have not reached interior optima.

## Fairer next round

1. Separate **mechanism tracks**: static scalar response; density/refraction; distributed kernel; arrangement/exterior geometry; motion/current coupling; time/history. Do not force all into the same one-dimensional proxy and call that complete coverage.
2. Give each eligible static track a comparable declared structural budget: amplitude rule, length rule, central behavior and outer behavior, with matched complexity control. Record attempted, failed and blocked formulas separately.
3. Apply the same baryonic nuisance ensemble and whole-galaxy/survey transfer tests to every candidate. Keep formal equations and parameter settings as separate inventory counts.
4. For spatial/motion/history tracks, finish their specific source or observability requirements. A blocked test is unfinished work, not evidence against the mechanism.
5. Treat this dataset as development. Select a final frozen model before genuinely independent observations are scored. Strong performance here motivates that next step; it cannot provide it retroactively.

This audit does not recommend another clock-only sweep. The immediate imbalance to repair is the lack of equivalent real-source tests for distributed geometry, density response and velocity-dependent alternatives, alongside the shared source/noise shortcomings.
