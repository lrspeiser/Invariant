# Curved backgrounds expose a conditional short-wavelength instability

For a perturbation with direction n on a curved auxiliary background, the leading static transfer coefficient is

    delta Phi / delta psi ~ ell² B(n) k²,
    B(n) = P_h + 2 ell² P_hh (n.H.n)² / a0².

The extra term vanishes on the constant-gradient backgrounds previously studied. It need not vanish on a physical mass distribution.

Evaluated this coefficient on finite positive Plummer backgrounds, across three action shapes, five radii, four lengths and radial/tangential directions. **38 of 120 sampled states have B>0.** Full local-flux perturbations independently approach the predicted coefficient for a selected positive-B case from each shape; at k*ell=10000 relative discrepancies are between 1.26e-7 and 2.32e-6 and decrease approximately as k^-2 over the three tested wave numbers. This checks the leading local coefficient, not a full time-dependent simulation.

Under standard local barotropic Euler dynamics, the leading density-mode growth rate satisfies

    sigma² ~ [4*pi*G*rho*ell²*B - c_s²] k².

For pressureless matter, B>0 therefore gives growth rates unbounded as k increases in this continuum approximation. This is stronger than ordinary wavelength-independent Jeans growth. Pressure can counter it if its sound-speed term exceeds the coefficient shown. Collisionless velocity dispersion requires a separate kinetic calculation. A static action alone does not establish those matter properties or a relativistic completion.

The largest sampled pressure threshold is c_s²=0.01932 in the chosen G=a0=source-scale=1 units, for shape 2, radius=scale, ell=scale and a tangential perturbation. This is a synthetic diagnostic, not a measured cluster limit.

Consequences: the original action is not certified dynamically safe. The positive-B backgrounds must remain in its assessment; they cannot be removed because other backgrounds give restoring waves. A viable completion would need justified dynamics, matter support or a derived short-distance limit. These calculations do not by themselves exclude an observed galaxy or cluster, establish a ghost, or rule out every completion of the action.

Evidence: `curved-background-symbol-001`, retaining all 120 states, the frozen action, nine full-flux checks and the explicit matter assumptions. No observational score or physical family exclusion is claimed.
