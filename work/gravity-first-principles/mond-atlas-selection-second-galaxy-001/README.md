# NGC3198 injection transfer: masks lose flux; template uncertainty still needs work

The second-galaxy experiment completed **9,558 injection trials and 8,262 known-template amplitude trials** without fitting observed galaxy velocities or tuning to eastern results. The stronger finding is that reasonable aggregate noise calibration does not guarantee calibrated uncertainty for a particular source template: real-background standardized amplitude-error mean squares range **1.002–3.208**, compared with **0.731–1.607** across the 32-draw conditional Gaussian controls. The most extreme real case has standardized mean error 0.651 and sample SD 1.717 across18 cores. This is a diagnostic requiring further testing, not a significance claim: templates share backgrounds, cores may be dependent, and fitted covariance/mean uncertainty was not propagated.

All162 cases fail the combined recovery flag. This must be interpreted at the actual injected strengths. The full detector's nominal5/10 amplitudes were normalized to the **native restored peak**, then expressed in detector-noise units; after spatial smoothing their actual maximum detector signal/noise is only **1.145–1.487** and **2.291–2.974**, respectively. They are not5/10 detector-peak SNR experiments. At the lower strength, the noiseless full detector correctly selects nothing under its2-sigma threshold. We preserve these outcomes rather than rescaling and rerunning after seeing failure.

At the stronger strength the full empirical detector retains **5.6–43.6%** of the true signal, with noiseless cases retaining4.7–32.2%. The full synthetic field captures99.996–99.999% of the reference flux, so this loss comes from threshold selection rather than the reported spatial crop. On the smaller native cores, stronger empirical injections retain68.4–74.4% of the signal inside the core; the core itself contains only75.9–80.0% of full signal flux. Full-flux and within-core denominators are reported separately. Peak detection alone is clearly insufficient for flux recovery. Paired full-detector morphology retention differences reach11.48 percentage points, with identical brightness/amplitude conventions and backgrounds; these templates have different velocity distributions, not inferred real galaxy motion.

## What was actually transferred

The24x24 covariance packet could not directly supply an81-square detector field, so a separate frozen geometry audit tested all38 existing core centers. **Nine western and seven eastern centers** support163-square real patches, allowing an81-square source field and41-pixel detector-convolution padding. All required pixels remain beyond the120-arcsec expanded complete MOM0 footprint, inside the42-pixel image-edge guard and in the appropriate hemisphere. The center remains in the original550–680arcsec annulus; the larger patch can extend beyond it. Every rejected center is retained. Larger patches overlap, and same-observation MOM0 screening is not independent certification of empty sky.

For those patches, the detector mean/variance was estimated only in the western fields and saved before eastern extraction. The full detector uses real surrounding background for30arcsec smoothing. No larger covariance was invented from the24-square model. The smaller-core analysis separately keeps the previously frozen NGC3198 DCT covariance, including its per-mode powers and DC correction, and runs all18 eastern cores plus32 conditional Gaussian draws. Its generalized least-squares estimator knows the exact template; this is an oracle amplitude benchmark, not blind morphology inference. Repeated amplitudes share the same estimator noise and are not independent replications.

Synthetic rotation, kinematic twist and streaming cases use matched elliptical brightness, three stored-channel centers, three positions, and two strengths. NGC3198's own header supplies the11.4311 by9.3625arcsec restoring beam at PA-80.43degrees and its own108-parent/72-stored-channel UVLIN mapping. Independent boxcar, full Hanning and decimated Hanning responses remain explicit alternatives; the exact correlator response and visibility-space continuum operator are not established. No NGC2976 channel operator was reused. [THINGS/Walter et al. 2008](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.2125) is the primary measurement source; [archive](https://things.www3.mpia.de/Data.html). Dirty-beam effects, CLEAN/residual scaling, primary beam, source-region covariance and publisher mask remain unresolved.

## Verification and retained failures

Pre-access controls passed continuum cancellation, independent run-mask logic, source flux/zero-motion identities, padding, beam normalization and scalar Gaussian GLS calibration. Independent replay then verified every8,262 GLS score and8,262 native-mask trial, plus144 full-detector trials using nine independently rendered source templates. It also read back all16 full real fields and independently refitted western detector statistics. Maximum score discrepancy is **7.11e-12**. The manual-cosine replay flagged individual GLS-weight entries at2.10e-9 relative against its initial1e-9 tolerance; that flag and the original checker failure remain visible even though score arithmetic passes. The receipt is explicitly `SCORE_REPLAY_PASS_WITH_WEIGHT_ENTRY_FLAG`.

The51.65-second numerical run finished all scores but failed its final operational private-storage check:108,130,496 bytes versus104,857,600 allowed by the initial packaging budget. This is not a scientific test failure. An interim compact-packet attempt is recorded, and the original packet was subsequently restored **byte-for-byte to its original SHA256**, including every GLS weight. `storage-restoration.json` supersedes that packaging disposition; the original run failure remains. All original scientific arrays and scores are retained, with the approximately3.27MB overage explicitly documented. Public output is approximately3.2MB; raw/derived background packets stay private. No new raw downloads, reserved objects, source velocity targets or observational gravity scores were accessed.

Useful next tests are prospectively declared, genuinely detector-peak-matched injections and stronger treatment of template-projected covariance uncertainty. Neither has been performed here, and no recovery correction is inferred from these failures. This package does not admit a galaxy cube likelihood.
